Heart attack or Stroke (women symptoms only!)?

Are you a woman and you can't tell if you're having a heart attack or a stroke because all of the symptoms we learn are catered to men, so answer a few questions to see if you're having a stroke or a heart attack!

Question 1

Is your pain spreading to your jaw, neck, and upper back, OR is your pain sudden in your head?

Pain is spreading to jaw, neck, and upper back Pain is in my head

Question 2

Is your breathing in hiccups OR do you have shortness of breath?

hiccups shortness of breath

Question 3

Are you experiencing facial drooping, slurred speech, confusion, or vision loss, OR are you lightheaded and fainting?

Facial drooping, slurred speech, etc Lightheaded

Question 4

Are you experiencing Extreme, unexplained fatigue or indigestion OR Sudden behavioral changes, agitation, or hallucinations?

sudden behavourial changes fatigue

Question 5

Are you experiencing Pressure, squeezing, or fullness in the center of the chest OR Sudden numbness or weakness, especially on one side of the body?

sudden weakness pressure in chest

Question 6

Are you experiencing flu like symptoms OR are you getting brain fog or memory loss?

flu like brain fog
